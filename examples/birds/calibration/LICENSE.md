# Licence of the calibration clips

Every clip in `audio/` is a 3 s excerpt of a xeno-canto recording, cut, downmixed to mono, resampled to 32 kHz
and re-encoded as 16-bit FLAC. Each clip keeps the licence of its recording, listed with the recordist and the
source in `ATTRIBUTION.csv`. Only CC0, CC BY and CC BY-SA recordings were used. Clips from CC BY-SA recordings
are redistributed under the same CC BY-SA licence.
