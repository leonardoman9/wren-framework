# Licence of the test clips

Species clips: stretches of xeno-canto recordings, downmixed to mono, resampled to 32 kHz and re-encoded as 16-bit
FLAC; each keeps the licence of its recording (CC0, CC BY or CC BY-SA only), listed with the recordist and the source
in `ATTRIBUTION.csv`. Clips from CC BY-SA recordings are redistributed under the same CC BY-SA licence.
Non-bird clips: the first 30 s of CC0 Freesound sounds (HQ previews), mono, 32 kHz, 16-bit FLAC; CC0 needs no
attribution, the source is listed anyway.
